# 14_cost10

**Question:** Do three pursuers catch more when a `coll_pp` costs 10, in place of 30?
**Differs from** `09_critic_input`, the two runs with `normalize_obs=false` (`2d0b98`, `6fce56`): a `coll_pp` costs 10.
**Runs:** MetaCentrum, 2 runs, 2026-11-10, commit `c8d89d5`: `a573b5` (seed 0), `052a62` (seed 1).

- Why: in `12_freeze`, all three pursuers on FRPN score above the learned policy at a cost of 10, and below it at 30.
