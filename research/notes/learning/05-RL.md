THE LOSS — one minibatch, loss_fn in swarm/learn/ppo.py

    loss = policy_loss  +  vf_coef × value_loss  −  ent_coef × entropy
           ~1              ~1 after reward normalization      small, ent_coef = 0.001

policy_loss    make good actions more likely, but do not move far
               ratio = exp(new logp − old logp)     1.0 = policy unchanged
               A     = advantage, standardized to mean 0 spread 1
               loss  = −mean( min( ratio × A,  clip(ratio, 1±clip_eps) × A ) )
               the clip is why the same box can be reused update_epochs times

value_loss     teach the critic to guess the return
               loss = ½ mean( (V − target)² )       target = advantage + old V
               squares the error, so the reward scale lands here — this is the
               term reward normalization exists to keep near 1

entropy        keep the policy from going deterministic too early
               entropy of a Gaussian depends on log_std only, not on the state
               ent_coef = 0.001, a small push. A larger one can break the std,
               see the next part

Every mean is masked: dead drones do not contribute. _mean(x, mask).


WHY THE ENTROPY BONUS CAN BREAK THE STD

    H = entropy    m = mean    s = std    z = the draw    u = (z − m)/s
    A = advantage  ρ = E[A·u], how strongly the advantage follows the noise

PPO shrinks s by itself once precision pays: that is E[A(u² − 1)] < 0.
The policy's own pull on m is about ρ/s. The bonus pulls the other way,
and how it pulls depends on how the action is bounded.

clip           ours: the action is clip(m + s·noise, −1, 1)
               ∂H/∂log s = +1, a constant that never decays, so noise pays for ever
               ∂H/∂m = 0, so the mean is free to grow with s
               runs with this bound kept m/s ≈ 1 and still flew

tanh squash    Gavin's: the action is tanh(z)
               ∂H/∂log s ≈ 1 − 1.6·s flips sign above s ≈ 0.63, which fixes the clip's
               problem. But a second term appears: ∂H/∂m ≈ −2·sign(m), a constant
               pull of the mean to zero, worth 0.02 at ent_coef = 0.01
               it crosses the policy's own pull ρ/s near s = 15
               past that the entropy term controls the mean, m/s → 0, and because
               the rate loop averages the command, the drone flies
               E[tanh z] ≈ 0.8·m/s ≈ 0

At ent_coef = 0.001 the pull on the mean is ten times weaker, so it crosses
the policy's own pull near s = 150, not s = 15.
